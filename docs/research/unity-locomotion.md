# Unity movement and aim authority for roomscale injection

Research for [Which Unity movement and aim mechanisms can preserve authority and collision during roomscale injection?](https://github.com/JakeGreen2145/UGEVR/issues/21), a prerequisite for [Which aiming and collision-aware roomscale behaviors can generalize across Unity player controllers?](https://github.com/JakeGreen2145/UGEVR/issues/11). Checked 2026-10-13. This report establishes source contracts and proposed falsification checks. It makes no product-policy decision or runtime compatibility claim.

## Result and evidence boundary

Unity has useful movement and geometry APIs, but their names do not identify the game's local player, movement owner, aim owner or shot origin. An adapter can preserve authority only after it identifies the actual game movement and aim paths and their timing. Collision-constrained body movement and tracked-head wall behavior are separate problems. The source evidence supports testing such adapters; it does not establish a universal movement hook or general impossibility.

The engine references below use Unity 2022.3 documentation and [UnityCsReference commit 91471827ee2cab002d877b827d67d120c09ba9d5](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/UnityCsReference/tree/91471827ee2cab002d877b827d67d120c09ba9d5). The inspected public controller is [Standard Assets Characters commit 8f185aa3c782d824976653dd6a6476312daad241](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/Standard-Assets-Characters/tree/8f185aa3c782d824976653dd6a6476312daad241), whose [project version is Unity 2019.3.0f1](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/Standard-Assets-Characters/blob/8f185aa3c782d824976653dd6a6476312daad241/ProjectSettings/ProjectVersion.txt). These different versions are explicit. No game, editor, physics simulation or headset test ran for this report. No installation or runtime configuration changed.

## Movement contracts

| Mechanism | Established contract | Limit for injection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `CharacterController.Move` | Accepts an absolute displacement, constrains it through collisions and returns collision directions. It applies no gravity. | Flags do not return accepted displacement. Read the actual before/after pose at the movement seam. The game's gravity, input, grounding and callbacks still need their owner. |
| Kinematic `Rigidbody.MovePosition` | The 2022.3 API describes world-space kinematic movement in fixed-update timing, with interpolation support. | It promises no sweep-and-slide rejection of an obstructed target. Kinematic bodies are controlled by script or animation and are not moved by forces or collisions. |
| Dynamic Rigidbody | Forces accumulate for the next physics simulation; force modes have distinct mass/time behavior. Velocity is world-space. | An impulse or target velocity is not an immediately accepted spatial displacement. Sampling the Transform before simulation or interpolated presentation can misattribute the result. |
| Custom motor | Public Standard Assets uses a custom capsule motor, kinematic Rigidbody, explicit casts and platform/gravity bookkeeping. | Identifying its Rigidbody alone does not identify the movement algorithm. Other shipped games may have entirely different motors. |

Sources for the table: [Move](https://docs.unity3d.com/2022.3/Documentation/ScriptReference/CharacterController.Move.html), [MovePosition](https://docs.unity3d.com/2022.3/Documentation/ScriptReference/Rigidbody.MovePosition.html), [isKinematic](https://docs.unity3d.com/2022.3/Documentation/ScriptReference/Rigidbody-isKinematic.html), [AddForce](https://docs.unity3d.com/2022.3/Documentation/ScriptReference/Rigidbody.AddForce.html), [velocity](https://docs.unity3d.com/2022.3/Documentation/ScriptReference/Rigidbody-velocity.html), and the custom controller evidence below.

`CharacterController.isGrounded` describes contact during the last move. Its capsule also has slope, step, skin-width and minimum-motion settings. These settings qualify any collision claim: skin permits limited penetration, and minimum-motion filtering can reject small requests. A second injector move can change last-move results that the game reads. This is an integration risk to measure, not proof that two calls always fail. [Grounded API](https://docs.unity3d.com/2022.3/Documentation/ScriptReference/CharacterController-isGrounded.html), [component manual](https://docs.unity3d.com/2022.3/Documentation/Manual/class-CharacterController.html).

The pinned C# bindings expose `Move`, `MovePosition` and collision queries through native engine calls. They establish signatures, not the full solver implementation or availability in a particular stripped player. Reference assemblies or an IL2CPP wrapper do not themselves prove that the game retained the method and native engine functionality. Capability and retained-method probes remain necessary. [Physics bindings, including Rigidbody and CharacterController](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/UnityCsReference/blob/91471827ee2cab002d877b827d67d120c09ba9d5/Modules/Physics/ScriptBindings/Dynamics.bindings.cs#L96).

## Queries and head contact

`Rigidbody.SweepTest` predicts collision for attached primitive or convex colliders. It returns a hit without moving the body. Concave attached meshes cannot supply its swept shape. `CapsuleCast` supports layer and trigger filtering but excludes colliders that overlap the capsule at its starting pose. A cast alone therefore cannot resolve all starting overlaps or implement stepping, slope rules, pushing, grounding or the game's triggers. [SweepTest](https://docs.unity3d.com/2022.3/Documentation/ScriptReference/Rigidbody.SweepTest.html), [CapsuleCast](https://docs.unity3d.com/2022.3/Documentation/ScriptReference/Physics.CapsuleCast.html).

`ComputePenetration` returns a separation direction and distance for two colliders at supplied poses, without moving them. At least one collider must be a box, sphere, capsule or convex mesh. It ignores backfaced triangles. This is a candidate tool for overlap handling, not a complete movement controller. Transform edits may also need explicit synchronization before physics queries; `SyncTransforms` flushes those edits into physics. It is not physics advancement. [ComputePenetration](https://docs.unity3d.com/2022.3/Documentation/ScriptReference/Physics.ComputePenetration.html), [SyncTransforms](https://docs.unity3d.com/2022.3/Documentation/ScriptReference/Physics.SyncTransforms.html).

Inference: the player's collision capsule can stop while the live tracked eyes continue through a wall relative to that capsule. A separate head shape/query and a defined visual or positional response are needed to address that case. Choosing a wall response is a human product decision. Reusing the body's masks and triggers blindly is unproven, especially for thin geometry, ignored self-colliders, doors and meshes without collision.

## Public controller evidence

`OpenCharacterController` sets its Rigidbody kinematic and disables Rigidbody gravity. Its own `MoveInternal` performs movement, updates grounding, measures resulting displacement and broadcasts collision events. It also preserves certain collision/velocity state around internal slope sliding. A raw Transform write bypasses those operations; a second public move overwrites some recorded move state. This is a concrete counterexample to treating every kinematic Rigidbody as the movement owner. Its `IsLocalHuman` setting selects extra accuracy work, not network authorization. [OpenCharacterController, initialization and movement](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/Standard-Assets-Characters/blob/8f185aa3c782d824976653dd6a6476312daad241/Assets/_Standard%20Assets/Characters/Scripts/Physics/OpenCharacterController.cs#L1094).

The surrounding adapter adds gravity and platform displacement, updates platform state after movement and resets upward velocity on ceiling contact. Platform rotation can also feed camera look input. Calling the lower-level capsule method alone would bypass parts of this bookkeeping. [CharacterBrain controller adapter](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/Standard-Assets-Characters/blob/8f185aa3c782d824976653dd6a6476312daad241/Assets/_Standard%20Assets/Characters/Scripts/Common/CharacterBrain.cs#L410).

`FirstPersonBrain.FixedUpdate` copies main-camera yaw into body rotation before movement. Its crouch/sprint/walk transitions change movement state, with some transitions deferred until grounded. Thus a camera change can influence gameplay, and lowering a rendered eye does not establish gameplay crouching. [FirstPersonBrain](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/Standard-Assets-Characters/blob/8f185aa3c782d824976653dd6a6476312daad241/Assets/_Standard%20Assets/Characters/Scripts/FirstPerson/FirstPersonBrain.cs#L163).

The demo camera controller expects exactly one FirstPersonBrain and disables itself when discovery is ambiguous. This single-player assumption is not a general local-player discovery rule. Unity's `Camera.main` means a tagged enabled camera, not player or shot authority. [Demo camera controller](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/Standard-Assets-Characters/blob/8f185aa3c782d824976653dd6a6476312daad241/Assets/_Standard%20Assets/Characters/Scripts/FirstPerson/FirstPersonCameraController.cs#L112), [Camera.main](https://docs.unity3d.com/2022.3/Documentation/ScriptReference/Camera-main.html).

Inference: controller or headset aim can supply a world-space direction, but actual firing may use another Transform, weapon offset, animation, spread, raycast, projectile or remote authority. Rotating a camera or weapon model proves visual aiming only. A game adapter must identify the shot calculation and preserve the game's collision, firing state and ownership. No inspected source establishes a universal Unity aim or local-player API.

## Timing and coordinate reconciliation

Unity allows zero or multiple fixed updates during a rendered frame. Different objects' same-event callback order is generally unspecified. `LateUpdate` follows `Update`; this does not identify every game's final movement or camera write. Custom PlayerLoop scheduling is possible, but the adapter must verify its position relative to the game's actual owner. [Execution order](https://docs.unity3d.com/2022.3/Documentation/Manual/ExecutionOrder.html), [pinned PlayerLoop declarations](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/UnityCsReference/blob/91471827ee2cab002d877b827d67d120c09ba9d5/Runtime/Export/PlayerLoop/PlayerLoop.bindings.cs).

`Physics.Simulate` advances physics and its contact/trigger callbacks even though it does not invoke `FixedUpdate`. Calling it for each eye would advance gameplay physics. Eye rendering must not be used to consume roomscale displacement or run movement again. Observe legitimate fixed steps separately from eye renders. [Simulate](https://docs.unity3d.com/2022.3/Documentation/ScriptReference/Physics.Simulate.html).

The following is geometric reasoning, not an adopted or tested algorithm. Let body anchor `b`, tracking-origin offset `o`, tracked head position `h` and calibrated tracking-to-world mapping `R` compose a head position as `c = b + R(h - o)`. When the game accepts a roomscale body displacement `a`, increasing `b` by `a` and increasing `o` by `R^-1 a` leaves the current head position unchanged. This prevents counting the same physical step once in the body and again in the tracked offset. Rejected motion remains relative to the body and needs the separate head-contact policy.

The adapter must attribute accepted motion to the requested roomscale operation. A later pose difference may include game input, gravity, platforms or networking corrections. Requests also need identity and consumption tracking across fixed steps; retrying an old blocked displacement as if it were fresh head movement is not justified. Teleports, respawns, player replacement, recentering and scale changes require explicit basis/lifetime handling so a discontinuity does not become a new locomotion request. Platform translation/rotation belongs to game movement; it must not masquerade as physical tracking motion. These are proposed invariants, not established behavior in shipped games.

## Small falsifiable implementation probes

1. For a retained CharacterController, request a measured horizontal displacement on flat ground, into a wall, up a step and on a slope. Record pre/post authoritative poses, collision flags, grounded state and game gravity before and after the integration point. Fail if later game movement overwrites or duplicates the request, or if existing jump/grounding changes without the declared mode.
2. Repeat with a kinematic custom motor and a dynamic Rigidbody. Record the game movement call, physics steps and authoritative accepted motion. Include initial overlap, thin obstacles, self-colliders, triggers and a moving obstacle. A successful sweep prediction alone is not a passing movement test.
3. Replay a known tracking trace while the body accepts all, some or none of the request. Verify coordinate reconciliation does not double the head displacement, does not turn rejected motion into repeated fresh input and reports separate body/head contact results. Test corner entry, leaning over an edge and physical crouching against the selected policy.
4. Hold tracking steady through a translating/rotating platform, teleport, respawn and scene/player replacement. Verify game-driven changes are not interpreted as physical walking, stale handles are rejected and recalibration follows the selected policy.
5. Change visual aim while logging the actual shot origin/direction and game firing state. Include weapon switching, obstruction close to the muzzle and a nonlocal player or AI. Fail if the injector changes another actor, bypasses the game's shot/collision rules or reports visual alignment as gameplay proof.
6. Compare normal rendering with two-eye rendering using counters at movement, physics, firing and collision callbacks. The eyes must not consume the same input twice. Verify that late pose presentation does not leak an eye-specific camera into the next gameplay update.

These checks can begin in controlled non-VR fixtures and then run in independent games under the accepted short functional gate. Source availability, fixture results, shipped-player compatibility and physical headset evidence must remain separate records. There is no runtime result in this report.
